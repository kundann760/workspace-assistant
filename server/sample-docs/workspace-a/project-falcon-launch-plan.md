# Project Falcon — Launch Plan

Project Falcon is Acme Corp's new drone-delivery service for small parcels under 2.5 kg.
This document is the single source of truth for the launch.

## Timeline

- Internal beta: 3 March 2026 with 40 employees in Pune.
- Public pilot: 14 May 2026 in three neighbourhoods of Pune (Baner, Aundh and Kothrud).
- General availability: planned for 1 September 2026, subject to DGCA approval.

## Secret launch code word

The internal launch code word for Project Falcon is **BLUE-PELICAN-42**.
Only the launch team should use it, on the go/no-go call.

## Team

- Launch owner: Priya Raman (Head of Operations).

- Regulatory contact: Dr. Anil Deshpande, who handles all DGCA correspondence.

## Budget

The approved pilot budget is ₹4.2 crore, of which ₹1.1 crore is reserved for insurance
and ₹0.6 crore for community outreach.

## Risks

1. Monsoon weather may ground flights for up to 30% of days in July and August.
2. Battery supplier VoltCell has a 10-week lead time, so orders must be placed by 15 March 2026.
3. Noise complaints: the drones must stay under 60 dB at 30 m altitude.
