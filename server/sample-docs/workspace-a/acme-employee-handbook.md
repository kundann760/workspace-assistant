# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (2026)

## Working hours

Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Outside core hours employees may work flexibly.
Fridays are meeting-free.

## Leave policy

- Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid leave per calendar year.
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year.
- Parental leave is 26 weeks for the birthing parent and 12 weeks for the non-birthing parent.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written
approval from a director. The home-office allowance is ₹30,000 once every two years.

## Expenses

Expenses above ₹10,000 need pre-approval from your manager. Submit receipts within 30 days
through the Expensify portal.

## Security

Never share customer data over personal email or messaging apps. Report suspected phishing to
security@acme.example within 1 hour.
