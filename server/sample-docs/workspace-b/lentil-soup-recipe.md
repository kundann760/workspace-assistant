# Grandma's Red Lentil Soup

Serves 4. Prep 15 minutes, cook 35 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup red lentils (masoor dal), rinsed
- 1 onion, 2 carrots and 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 5 cups vegetable stock
- Juice of 1 lemon and a handful of coriander

## Method

1. Sauté the onion and carrots in olive oil for 6 minutes, add garlic and spices for 1 minute.
2. Add lentils and stock. Simmer 25 minutes until the lentils collapse.
3. Blend half the soup for a creamy texture, stir in lemon juice and top with coriander.

## Grandma's secret

The secret ingredient is a teaspoon of tamarind paste stirred in at the very end.

## Storage

Keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.
