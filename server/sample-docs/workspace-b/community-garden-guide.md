# Riverside Community Garden — Member Guide

## Plots

There are 48 raised-bed plots, each 1.2 m × 3 m. Annual membership is ₹2,400 per plot and
includes water and shared tools.

## Watering schedule

Automatic drip irrigation runs at 6:00 and 18:00 every day from March to October.
During the monsoon (June to September) the evening cycle is switched off.

## Composting

Green waste goes in the left bin, brown waste (dry leaves, cardboard) in the right bin.
Compost is turned every Saturday at 8:00 by the volunteer of the week.

## Pest control

Only organic methods are allowed: neem oil spray, marigold borders and hand-picking.
Chemical pesticides result in immediate loss of the plot.

## Garden committee

The committee chair is Farah Siddiqui. Plot disputes go to the committee meeting on the
first Sunday of every month at 10:00.
